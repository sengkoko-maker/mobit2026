Mobit Ong is the Founder of Mobit Solution and a System Integration Specialist with a degree in Information Technology from the University of Southern Queensland (USQ).

His experience extends beyond computers. He brings IT systems, networking, CCTV, alarm, smart door access and ANPR systems together into one practical, reliable solution for each customer.

By combining academic knowledge, hands-on field experience and business understanding, he helps factories, offices and SMEs build systems that are stable, secure, easy to manage and ready to grow.
